## Why BlackJack??

My 10-year-old laptop with 4 GB of RAM and a 256 GB SSD was failing to handle even the simplest tasks, like running a browser with just two tabs open. It was my semester break, and I wanted to work on something. But being handicapped, I couldn’t take on a big project. So, I ended up making this game in **VIM**. I had to create the whole thing without any assistance from the internet or AI. It wasn’t too hard since the concept was fairly simple. The hardest part was figuring out the rules of Blackjack. Thanks to **[Washington Post's Black Jack Web Game](https://games.washingtonpost.com/games/blackjack)**, I was able to play the game over and over and get a grasp of the rules.
